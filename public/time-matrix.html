TIME Matrix for Technology Portfolio Rationalization

Purpose: Strategic assessment of each technology/application to determine investment disposition

X-Axis (Tech Health): Overall technical health (1-3: Significant Issues → Healthy & Aligned)

Y-Axis (Strategic Alignment): Alignment with BCI technology direction (1-3: Poor → Excellent)

Bubble Size: Configurable dimension (operational health, strategic value, etc.)

Quadrant Actions:

TOLERATE (Top-Left) - Low health + High alignment → Monitor but keep
INVEST (Top-Right) - High health + High alignment → Strategic investment
ELIMINATE (Bottom-Left) - Low health + Low alignment → Phase out
MIGRATE (Bottom-Right) - High health + Low alignment → Replace/upgrade
